TapNova — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains the categories of information that may be processed when you use the TapNova Telegram Mini App and related game services (the “Service”). The exact information processed depends on the features you use.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Telegram/account information: Telegram user identifier and information made available to the Mini App for authentication.
  • Gameplay information: game progress, taps, points, missions, referrals, rewards and related activity.
  • Wallet and transaction information: public wallet address, payment amount, transaction reference and payment status when you choose to connect a wallet or use a blockchain payment feature.
  • Technical information: basic request, device or diagnostic information processed by the platforms used to operate and secure the Service.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to provide the game, authenticate users, save progress, operate rewards and referrals, process and verify requested payments, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors and maintain service security.

3. Service providers and blockchain

The Service relies on third-party platforms, which may include Telegram, Cloudflare and TON wallet or blockchain infrastructure. Information necessary for a feature may be processed by those providers under their own terms and privacy policies. Public blockchain transactions and wallet addresses may be visible to anyone and cannot generally be made private or erased by TapNova.

4. Sharing

Information may be made available to infrastructure or service providers where needed to operate the Service, or where disclosure is required by applicable law. Do not submit information that you do not want processed by the relevant platform or made public on a blockchain.

5. Retention and security

Information is retained for as long as needed to operate the relevant game features, maintain records, resolve disputes and meet applicable obligations. No online service can guarantee absolute security; please protect your Telegram account and wallet credentials.

6. Your choices

You can stop using the Service and disconnect your wallet through your wallet application. Some game records or public blockchain data may remain after you stop using the Service, where deletion is technically unavailable or records must be retained.

7. Children

The Service is not intended for children who are not permitted to use Telegram or the relevant wallet services under applicable age requirements.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the Service changes. The updated date above indicates the latest revision. For privacy questions, contact the TapNova team through the official bot: t.me/tapNovaEarnBot.
