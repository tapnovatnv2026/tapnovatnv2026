TapNova Privacy Policy

TapNova may process Telegram-provided account identifiers and app interaction data to operate gameplay, referrals, rewards, and account features.

If you connect a TON wallet or initiate a payment, the app may process the wallet address, payment intent, transaction details, and verification status to complete and verify the requested transaction.

Do not share wallet recovery phrases or private keys. TapNova does not need them.

Data may be retained as needed to operate the service, prevent abuse, maintain records, and resolve support issues. Public blockchain transactions may be visible on the TON network independently of TapNova.

For privacy questions, contact the official TapNova Telegram bot.
